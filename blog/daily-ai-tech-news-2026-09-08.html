Mistral raised €3B at over €21B valuation, with Samsung leading the Series D. That is the biggest tech fundraise in Europe so far, and it puts Mistral's open-weight, private-compute stack firmly in the center of the European AI race.

Oxford researchers published proof that gravity operates at quantum scale. The work opens new paths for quantum gravity theories, not just more precise measurement.

A legacy Certificate Authority from the 90s had its RSA keys factored. Old crypto is not theoretical risk. It is a live vulnerability sitting in infrastructure that nobody has rebuilt.

Fresh papers: WorldSculpt builds interactive 3D worlds from video, and UniMate unifies animation across different skeleton types and clothing.
